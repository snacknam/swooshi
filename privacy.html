Swooshi — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026 · Developer: SnackNam · Contact: kwansiknam@gmail.com

Swooshi is a block puzzle game. This policy explains what the game collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we do not collect

We do not ask for or collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. There are no accounts.

Data stored on your device

Your scores, coins, items, missions, achievements, settings (such as sound) and purchases are stored only on your device. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Gameplay analytics

To improve the game, the app sends gameplay events (for example: a game started or ended, the score, which booster was used, whether a purchase went through or was cancelled) to our analytics provider, PostHog. Each install gets a random identifier that is not linked to your identity. We also send the app version and platform (iOS or Android). We ask PostHog not to look up your location from your IP address. We do not use this data for advertising and we do not sell it.

Advertising

The free version shows ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect your device's advertising identifier, IP address (used for approximate location), and device information. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

  • If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK, or Switzerland, the app asks for your consent before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice any time with "Privacy options" in Settings.
  • On iOS, the app asks for permission before tracking (App Tracking Transparency). If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will not be personalized.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

Buying "Remove Ads" turns off ads between games. Ads you choose to watch (for example, to continue a game or get a booster) stay available.

Purchases

Purchases ("Remove Ads", coin packs and the Starter Pack) are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your payment details. The game only learns what was bought so it can deliver it.

Children

Swooshi is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

  • Turn off ad personalization: "Privacy options" in Settings (EEA/UK), decline tracking on iOS, or reset your advertising ID on Android.
  • Delete local data: uninstall the app.
  • Questions or a request to delete analytics data: email kwansiknam@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above.
